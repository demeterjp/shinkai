# Privacy Policy – Shinkai

Shinkai does not collect, share or sell any personal data, and it has no server.

Everything the app remembers is saved only on your device and is never sent anywhere: your practice progress (which characters you got wrong and how long you took), your streak, and your settings (theme, hints and vibration). The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and needs no internet connection.

The app opens your email app or a web page only if you choose to send feedback or open this policy. What you send then is up to you.

If you uninstall the app or use "Reset progress" in Settings, this data is deleted.

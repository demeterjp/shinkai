# Privacy Policy – Shinkai

Shinkai does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal data.

All practice progress (which characters you got wrong and how long you took) is saved only on your device and is never sent anywhere. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and needs no internet connection.

If you uninstall the app, this data is deleted.

# Shinkai (深海)

Learn to type Japanese kana on the phone flick keyboard, until it becomes automatic.

</div>

---

## What it is

Shinkai trains you to type hiragana and katakana on the flick keyboard used on phones in Japan. Instead of flashcards, you practice on the keyboard itself: see a character, type it, repeat, until your fingers know where everything is without thinking.

## Features

- Built-in flick keyboard: tap for the main character, swipe left, up, right or down for the others
- Learn the keyboard: a short guide to all 10 keys and the vowel pattern behind them
- Hiragana rounds: 10 random characters or all 46
- Words: type whole words, one kana at a time
- Extra: Katakana, and a blind keyboard with empty keys, so you have to remember where every character is
- Characters you struggle with come up more often in rounds of 10 and in Words
- Practice weakest and a map of your keyboard: see which characters you know well
- Hints on the keys that you can switch off
- Daily streak with a calendar and a streak freeze that protects one missed day
- Sounds and haptic feedback, both can be switched off in Settings
- Several themes
- Works offline, no account, no ads, no tracking
